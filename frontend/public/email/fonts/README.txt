Fonts for the Head of Family emails (frontend/public/email/fonts).

DM Sans (Bold 700) and Manrope (variable, weights 400 to 700): Latin subset only, WOFF2.
Source: Google Fonts, fetched 19 Nov 2026. Licence: SIL Open Font License 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org), which allows
self-hosting and redistribution. Copyright the DM Sans Project Authors and the Manrope Project Authors.

They are served from our own domain, next to the email pictures, so opening an email never contacts a third party.
Most mail programs ignore web fonts and use the fallback stack in email_templates.py; Apple Mail and iOS Mail load these.
