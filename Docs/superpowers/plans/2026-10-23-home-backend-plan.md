## Risks

Holdings cache is per web worker (three workers, 15-minute life). Sector view depends on disclosure freshness (about 10 days old, up to 40), hence the label. A wrong stale-price rule could show a price a day old (stored prices are up to about 10 hours behind between the evening NAV publish and 06:00). Retention growth (check size).
