Asset allocation

Shows the true equity, debt, gold and cash mix across all of a client's funds, taken from what each fund discloses, and leads into the cap split and stock list. Revised 2026-10-13: it is built after attribute 02, because its outer ring reuses 02's classification (F19).

The formula

Asset mix from what funds actually hold

The three fund weights are derived from the spec's result; the spec states only the four percentages.

asset class % = Σ over held funds ( fund weight × fund's % in that class ) from each fund's own disclosed rows, not its SEBI category Worked example (spec) fund weights 60% / 30% / 10% equity fund 95/0/0/5 · hybrid 65/30/0/5 · gold fund 0/0/100/0 (equity / debt / gold / cash) equity 0.60×95 + 0.30×65 = 76.5 debt 0.30×30 = 9.0 gold 0.10×100 = 10.0 cash 0.60×5 + 0.30×5 = 4.5 Rows with no ISIN (CBLO, TREPS, net receivables) are summed into their class across funds here. Attribute 06 keeps them apart. Opposite rules, on purpose.

Q26Decided

How is the hedged-arbitrage "cash-like" toggle built?

The issue

The spec says hedged arbitrage positions can be treated as cash-like, as a display toggle and not at parse time, because the raw disclosure does not reliably say which lines are hedged. But a toggle needs something to act on, and a line-level flag does not exist.

Why it matters

As written the toggle has no data behind it. Arbitrage funds also carry futures lines (see Q13), which are the other half of the hedge.

Options
  1. ADecide at fund level: a fund whose SEBI category is Arbitrage Fund moves wholly into cash-like when the toggle is on.
  2. BDrop the toggle in this release; arbitrage stays equity.
  3. CA, and apply the same to the Equity Savings category's hedged part (needs a rule nobody has written).
Recommended

A. Uses a field that exists (schemes.sebi_category) and matches how investors think of arbitrage funds.

After your answer
  • The toggle recomputes from the same stored rows, no extra ingestion.
Decided 2026-10-13

A confirmed. Fund-level toggle keyed on schemes.sebi_category == "Arbitrage Fund", not a per-holding instrument check — in Task 5 of the consumer plan.

Q27Decided

What is the outer ring of the donut for debt, gold and cash?

The issue

The spec describes a two-ring donut, inner ring asset class, outer ring "next level down", and chains the equity ring into attribute 02 then 06. Nothing says what the outer ring shows for debt, gold or cash. The disclosures carry a credit rating for debt lines but the parser does not capture it as its own field (Q9 notes the rating can be swallowed into sector_raw).

Why it matters

Without a rule, three of the four rings have no outer level and the chart looks unfinished.

Options
  1. AOuter ring only for equity (Large, Mid, Small, Other, from attribute 02). Other classes are one segment.
  2. BEquity as in A; debt by rating bucket (needs a rating field).
  3. CEquity as in A; debt split by fund (which fund holds the debt).
Recommended

A. Needs no new data. Rating can follow once Parse captures it.

After your answer
  • Rating capture is added to the deferred list.
Decided 2026-10-13

A confirmed. Outer ring is equity-only (Large/Mid/Small/Other from attribute 02); debt-rating detail stays flagged as cheap future work in DEFERRED_FEATURES.md, not built now.

F13Plan fix

The asset-class test depends on how Q8 and Q13 are answered

applied 2026-10-13
Applied
Consumer Task 5 now tests against the final 8-value enum (no OTHER backfill) and gains a test that derivative and other rows are excluded from every class sum and counted in the coverage note.
The problem

The attribute 13 test needs asset_type stamped from real section headers on each holding row. The current ingestion plan does not stamp it at all, and the consumer plan's Task 1 would add the column later with a backfill to OTHER, a value outside the six-value enum. It also treats every row inside "Equity" as equity, though arbitrage funds list futures and fund-of-funds list fund units there (Q13).

Why it matters

The three-fund worked example (76.5 / 9.0 / 10.0 / 4.5) would pass on synthetic rows and still be wrong on a real arbitrage fund.

The fix
  • After Q8 and Q13: rewrite Task 5's first test against the final enum.
  • Add a second test that derivative and unrecognised-section rows are excluded from every class sum and are counted in the coverage note.
Effect on the current system
  • None until built. Reads only scheme_holdings.
Effect on the plan
Task 5 gains one test and a reworded first test.

F19Plan fix

Attribute 13 cannot be built before attribute 02

found 2026-10-13, applied to the plan
The problem

The overview's suggested order was 13, then 02, then 10, on the reasoning that 13 shares the drill-down into 02. Q27 (decided) says attribute 13's outer ring splits the equity ring into Large, Mid, Small and Other by reusing attribute 02's classification directly. That classification is the market_cap_classifications table and join that Task 3 (attribute 02) creates.

Evidence
Consumer plan Task 5 and Q27's decided text.
Why it matters

Built first, attribute 13 would have no classification to read. Either its outer ring would be stubbed and redone, or the work would be pulled into the wrong task.

The fix
  • Order is now 06, 02, 13, 10. The consumer plan's order line and Task 5 heading say so, and the runs are renumbered.
  • Attribute 10 depends on neither and can go last.
Effect on the current system
  • None.
Effect on the plan
Run order only. No task changes content.
