Q23Decided

Overlap across all common instruments, or stocks only?

The issue

The formula is the sum of the smaller of two weights over common ISINs. The shared data holds every instrument with an ISIN. Two hybrid funds can share government bonds or the same deposit, and two debt funds can overlap heavily on paper that is not a stock.

Why it matters

The PDF's headline reads "Top 10 stocks" and its overlap example lists stocks. Counting bonds would raise the overlap of hybrid and debt pairs well above what an investor means by it.

Options
  1. AEquity rows only (domestic and international). A hybrid pair is measured on its equity part only.
  2. BAll instruments with an ISIN.
  3. CEquity rows only by default, with a toggle for all.
Recommended

A. Matches the PDF's wording. Debt overlap can be a later addition.

After your answer
  • compute_fund_pair_overlap filters on asset_type before the minimum.
  • The percentage is a share of each fund's net assets, so a fund with 70% equity can reach at most 70%. The UI says so.
Decided 2026-10-13

A confirmed. Equity-only overlap, consistent with Q17/Q27's equity-only scope for this attribute family — in Task 4 of the consumer plan.

Q24Decided

Where do the overlap bands live?

The issue

The spec gives 20% and 40% as suggested thresholds and says "configurable". Sub-project 1 settled that configurable values live in a small table edited in DBeaver, with no admin screen (ranking weights, scenario library).

Why it matters

A constant in code is the cheapest. A table follows the precedent but adds a migration for two numbers.

Options
  1. AConstants in the frontend and backend, no table.
  2. BA one-row config table like ranking_weights.
  3. CConstants now, move to a table only if you ask to change them.
Recommended

C. Two numbers do not earn a table yet. You can tell me to move them.

After your answer
  • One shared constants module, tested.
Decided 2026-10-13

C confirmed. Named constants, not a config table — no admin surface for two values that don't need runtime editing. Revisit as a table only if a change is actually requested.

Q25Decided

How many funds before the heatmap becomes unreadable?

The issue

The matrix grows with the square of the funds held. A household of 4 members with 10 funds each has 40 funds and 780 unordered pairs in the combined scope, all stored in the one JSON payload that every Analytics load fetches.

Why it matters

A 40 by 40 grid does not fit a phone, and the payload grows with it.

Options
  1. ACap at the 12 largest holdings by value per scope and say "top 12 funds by value" under the heatmap.
  2. BNo cap, horizontal scroll.
  3. CCap at 12 and add a "show all pairs above 20%" list underneath.
Recommended

C. Keeps the heatmap readable and the payload small, and the list still surfaces any high pair.

After your answer
  • The cap is applied in the compute step, so it also bounds stored size (see Q4).
Decided 2026-10-13

C confirmed. 12-fund cap on the heatmap plus a "pairs over 20%" list underneath, so the cap never silently hides a genuinely high overlap — in Task 4 of the consumer plan.
