What "cold" means for this page

  • First open after an import: the household recompute may still be running, so stored sections (score, benchmark_funds, overlap) can be missing for a while. Everything else must work without them.
  • First open after a deploy or on another worker: in-memory caches (holdings, category scores) are empty; the score's peer pass is paid again (seconds, unmeasured).
  • Weekend or holiday: no row dated today exists, so a naive holdings call re-downloads every held fund's NAV history. The Home staging run (14 Oct–23 Oct handoff, three households of 12 to 46 funds) measured a cold holdings call at 4.9 to 24.3 s for exactly this reason; warm holdings were 0 ms. This page would inherit that cost if it calls compute_holdings for the whole household to find one fund.
  • A single fund is cheap on its own: its NAV series, TRI series and holdings batch are indexed single-fund reads. The Home measurements put one bulk NAV read for 12 to 46 funds at 8 to 240 ms, so one fund is a fraction of that (an estimate, not measured).

7. Risk metrics (3Y): easy or heavy?

Verdict: easy, build it now (subject to A3)

  • Inputs: one fund's NAV for 3 years (about 750 rows) and one index's TRI for the same days. Both are single-key reads on tables we already fill daily.
  • Volatility: standard deviation of daily NAV returns, annualised (times the square root of 252). Max drawdown: largest peak-to-trough fall of the NAV in the window. Sharpe: annualised excess return over a risk-free rate divided by volatility. Beta: covariance with the index's daily returns divided by the index's variance.
  • Cost: arithmetic on about 750 numbers, a few milliseconds in Python (an estimate; not timed). No peers, no cold peer pass.
  • What is not trivial: the Sharpe risk-free rate does not exist in the code, so you must pick a number or source (A3); the bar labels need rules; funds with under 3 years of history show a shorter window or "not enough history" (rule to fix in the spec); NAV dates and index dates must be aligned before computing beta.
  • Mockup mix-up to fix: the page 26 scatter shows 18.7% return and 14.2% volatility for the fund, but 18.7% is the 5-year figure on page 24 and 14.2% is the 3-year volatility. Both axes must use the same 3-year window (the 3-year return in the mockup is 16.3%).
