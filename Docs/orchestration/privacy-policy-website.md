### 3.2 What is inside a CAS statement

A CAS contains the investor's name, PAN, the email and mobile number registered with the fund house, folio numbers, fund names, transaction history, units held, values and the distributor's code (ARN). We use this information only to build and analyse your portfolio. Statements may also list nominees. We do not use nominee details.

### 3.3 Information collected automatically

When you sign in, we record basic technical details:

- your IP address;
- your device type, operating system and browser; and
- a random device identifier stored by our app on your device.

We use these to keep your account secure, spot unusual sign-in activity, fix problems, and understand which devices our users use so we can support them well. We do **not** track your precise location, and we do not track what you do on other websites or apps.

### 3.4 Public market data

To value your portfolio and calculate analytics, we use publicly available market data such as fund NAVs, expense ratios and index levels, from sources including AMFI and the NSE. This is not personal data.

### 3.5 What we never ask for

We will never ask for your bank account details, card details, UPI PIN, Aadhaar number, broker or fund-house login passwords, or access to your contacts, photos or location.

> **Stay safe.** If anyone claiming to be from Unifolio asks you for these, or for an OTP, it is not us. Please report it to **[security@unifolio.in]**.

## 4. How we use your data and why we are allowed to

### 4.1 Purposes

We use your personal data only to:

1. create and secure your account and sign you in;
2. read your statements and build your holdings, returns and portfolio analytics;
3. show each family member's portfolio separately and as a combined family view;
4. match each statement to the right person using their PAN;
5. send you sign-in codes, security alerts and important account notices;
6. send you product updates and offers about Unifolio, where you have not opted out ([section 16](#16-updates-and-offers-from-us));
7. detect and prevent fraud, abuse and security incidents;
8. fix problems and improve the Service; and
9. comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.

We do not use your data for advertising, credit scoring, or any purpose not listed here. If we want to use it for a new purpose, we will ask you first.

### 4.2 Our legal basis: your consent

We process your personal data **on the basis of your consent**. You give it by a clear action, at the points below. There is a line of text sits next to the button you press, with links that open the full documents in a new tab.

| When | What you see | What counts as your agreement |
|---|---|---|
| **Signing up or signing in for the first time** (mobile OTP, email or Google) | Below the button: "By continuing, you agree to our Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy." | Pressing the button (for example **Get OTP** or **Continue**). |
| **Uploading a statement** | Near the upload button: "By continuing, you agree to our privacy policy." This opens the notice that explains how we handle the PAN in your statement. | Pressing **Upload**. We ask again on every upload. |
| **Coming back to a scheduled deletion** | Above the button: "By reactivating, you agree to our Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy." | Pressing **Reactivate**. |
| **After we update our documents** | A message, "We've updated our Terms", that you must answer before you can continue. | Pressing **Agree**. |

If the documents cannot be loaded, the button cannot record your agreement, and the screen tells you so and offers a retry.

In limited cases, the DPDP Act allows processing without fresh consent, for example to comply with a law, a court order or a request from an authorised government body, or to respond to a medical or safety emergency.

### 4.3 Withdrawing consent

You can withdraw your consent at any time. Because we need your data to run your portfolio, withdrawing consent means closing your account. Today, you do this by deleting your account:

1. Open **Profile** and choose **Delete account**. Tell us why you are leaving (optional feedback is stored without any link to you).
2. We record that you have **withdrawn consent for every purpose** covered by our documents, and we schedule your account for deletion.
3. You then have a **30-day grace period** to change your mind. Until then, you can sign back in and press **Reactivate**, which counts as giving your agreement again.
4. After 30 days, your account, family members, portfolio data and remaining statement files are permanently deleted ([section 12](#12-how-long-we-keep-your-data)).

There is no separate "withdraw consent" button that leaves your account open, because the Service cannot work without the data. [You can also ask us to start the deletion for you by writing to **privacy@unifolio.in** from the email address or phone number linked to your account.]

Withdrawing does not affect anything we lawfully did before you withdrew. Some data may still have to be kept where the law requires it (see [section 12](#12-how-long-we-keep-your-data)).

### 4.4 Records of your consent

Each time you agree or withdraw, we add an entry to a consent record. It shows:

- which document and which version you agreed to or withdrew from, with a fingerprint of the exact text you saw;
- the date and time, and which screen it happened on (for example sign-up, upload, reactivation or account deletion);
- your device type, browser and device identifier, and a **shortened** version of your IP address together with a protected one-way fingerprint of the full address. We do not store your full IP address in this record; and
- for an upload, a fingerprint of the statement file (not the file itself) and the upload it relates to.

These entries are **append-only**: once written, they cannot be changed or deleted, so they stay accurate if there is ever a dispute. This lets us show that consent was given or withdrawn, as the law requires. For now we keep them even after an account is deleted, and they hold no portfolio data. ## 5. Your PAN: how we handle it

Your PAN is the most sensitive thing we keep, so here is exactly how we handle it.

| | |
|---|---|
| **What we store** | The PAN of each person in your family whose statement you upload. We read it from the statement, or you type it when you complete a family member's details. |
| **Why** | Names on statements vary: initials, spellings, nicknames. A PAN lets us reliably put each statement under the right person. It also makes sure the same person's portfolio is not tracked in two different Unifolio accounts. |
| **How it is protected** | Each PAN is stored with its own strong encryption, on top of the encryption that protects our whole database. To check for a match, we compare a one-way fingerprint of the PAN that cannot be turned back into the PAN. The real PAN is unlocked only when genuinely needed. |
| **How it is shown** | Never in full. In the app you see only a masked version, such as **AB\*\*\*\*\*\*4F**. |
| **When we check it** | The moment you upload a statement, so we can tell you straight away whether it belongs to someone already in your family. If you cancel the upload or do not confirm it, any PAN we noted for that upload is released. |
| **What we never do** | Use it for credit checks, KYC or identity verification with outside parties; share it, except where the law requires ([section 8](#8-who-we-share-your-data-with)); or use it for marketing. |
| **When it is deleted** | Permanently, when you remove that family member or delete your account. Your own PAN stays for as long as your account exists, because your own portfolio depends on it. |

**One person, one account.** To keep data accurate and private, each person's PAN can be linked to only one Unifolio account. If you upload a statement for someone who is already tracked in another account, their funds from that statement can count in your family total, but we will not open their individual portfolio to you. We will never tell you which account they are in.

Your uploaded statement file also contains your PAN. That file is deleted after 30 days ([section 12](#12-how-long-we-keep-your-data)).

## 6. Your statement password

CAS statements are password-protected, and the password is usually your PAN. When you enter it, we use it once to open the file. It is **never saved** to our database or our files.

If you enter the wrong password, we keep the locked file in temporary memory for up to 15 minutes so you can try again without uploading it again. After that, it is cleared.

## 10. How we protect your data

We follow reasonable security practices appropriate to the sensitivity of the data we hold, as required by the SPDI Rules and the DPDP Act.

- All data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).
- Our databases and stored statement files are encrypted at rest, with keys managed through AWS's key management service.
- Your PAN has extra, separate encryption, and match checks use a protected one-way fingerprint rather than the PAN itself.
- Sign-in codes and session tokens are stored only in scrambled (hashed) form, so even we cannot read them.
- Sign-in codes expire after 5 minutes and lock after 5 wrong attempts.
- Access to production systems is limited to authorised team members who need it for their work.

## 13. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act, you have the right to:

| Right | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Access** | Get a summary of the personal data we hold about you, how we use it, and who we have shared it with. |
| **Correction and updating** | Have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected or completed. You can edit most of it directly in the app. |
| **Erasure** | Have your data deleted. You can delete a family member, an import or your whole account from inside the app, or ask us to do it. |
| **Withdraw consent** | Withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account. See [section 4.3](#43-withdrawing-consent). |
| **Grievance redressal** | Complain to our Grievance Officer and get a response ([section 21](#21-grievance-officer-and-complaints)). |
| **Nominate** | Name someone who can exercise these rights for you if you die or become unable to act. |
| **Complain to the regulator** | If you are not satisfied with our response, complain to the **Data Protection Board of India**. |

These rights are not absolute. The law allows us to decline or limit a request in some cases, for example where we must keep data to comply with a legal obligation. If we do, we will tell you why.
