Why section 00 is one box, not six: the PDF explicitly asks for the look-through calculation — Σ (fund weight in portfolio × security weight in that fund) — to be built once as a shared service. Attributes 02, 06, 10, 13 and 15 are different slices/filters of the same per-client stock table, not five separate computations.

05

Open questions — all 4 answered 2026-10-06

Previously framed as unresolved conflicts; full record in decisions.md's "Analytics PDF open questions (4 of 4 answered)" entry. One sequencing call remains open (flagged below), the rest are resolved.

Ranking formula (09)
Not a conflict — independent net-new feature on its own 5-factor formula, unrelated to Scorer v1/v2. Scorer v2 stays on hold pending all 11 components.
Benchmark basis (12)
TRI sourcing confirmed free & reachable via a sibling NSE endpoint (deep-dive done per explicit request) — small, well-scoped build. Still open: build now vs. schedule separately (sequencing only, not feasibility).
Stock P/E · P/B · sector source (15)
Resolved: build free in-house (NSE/BSE scraping + AMC factsheets), no paid-vendor exception.
AMC disclosure format survey
Spike 1 done: SEBI-prescribed format confirmed (ISIN, Industry, Quantity, Market Value, % to NAV regulatorily mandated) — not 40+ ad hoc formats. Still open: no single aggregated feed, file URLs not yet confirmed machine-fetchable — spike 2 (pull/diff 8-10 real files) recommended next.
