Trending topics to Unifolio feature candidates

Research run of 16 Nov 2026. Research only: nothing built, committed or pushed. Four parallel research passes (investor pain points, finfluencer topics, competitor landscape, regulatory and tax triggers), merged and checked against DEFERRED_FEATURES.md.

1. Read this first: what this is and is not

Reddit, Instagram and X could not be read. Reddit was blocked on every route (page fetch, search, built-in browser). Instagram and X sit behind logins. So there is no raw social-media data here. What you have is what Indian finance media, AMCs, brokers, forums such as Freefincal, Value Research and ValuePickr, and a few indexed posts say investors ask about. That is supply-side coverage, not measured demand.

No per-video view counts for 2026 could be verified. "Rank" below is my judgment of how often a topic appears across independent sources, not a measured share of content. Nearly every regulatory item comes from secondary press, because no SEBI, AMFI or CBDT document could be opened. Verify each against the primary circular before building.

A real demand check still needs a person to read r/IndiaInvestments, r/IndianMutualFunds and r/personalfinanceindia by hand, or a logged-in browser pass (section 8).

5. Compliance guardrails

  • Unifolio is not an RIA. Every feature above should show facts about the user's own portfolio and never say "switch", "sell" or "buy". The scenario simulator's existing release gates (markets review, SEBI disclaimer) are the model.
  • SEBI rules for finfluencers (registration number on posts from 1 May 2026; regulated entities barred from tying up with unregistered influencers) matter if Unifolio ever works with creators for acquisition. Share-card features that go viral should avoid performance claims.
  • Educational content from unregistered people may not include buy, sell or hold calls or return claims. A "claim checker" ("test this viral claim on your portfolio") is shareable but needs a compliance read before building.

8. Instagram spot check (added later, small sample)

Very thin evidence. One read-only look at three hashtag grids (#mutualfunds, #sipinvestment, #personalfinanceindia) in a signed-in browser on 16 Nov 2026. The grids show thumbnails and on-image text only: no view counts, dates or captions, and the mix is algorithmic and personalised to that account. Treat this as colour, not ranking. Reddit was blocked in Chrome too, and X showed only its logged-out sign-in page, so neither was read.

  • Beginner SIP content dominates. On-image text included "My monthly ₹5,000 SIP", "Want to start SIP but don't know where to begin? (15 minutes)", "How to start?", "If I had to invest my first ₹10,000", "Financial mistakes in your 20s", "Mutual funds explained like you're 15", and a "Types of mutual funds" carousel. This matches the SIP and first-investment topics in the other lenses.
  • Return-projection graphics. One post showed a table of monthly SIPs across index, small-cap, mid-cap and gold funds ending in "5K monthly SIP grows to 3.73 Cr". This is a shareable format a tracker could offer as a calculator.
  • Ranked best-fund tables. One post listed top funds by category for July 2026 with 3- and 5-year returns. This format is closest to what SEBI's finfluencer rules restrict, so Unifolio should not copy it.
  • Family framing. "5 financial numbers every family should know" came from an AMFI-registered distributor account. It lines up with your household feature.
  • Contrarian hooks. "Stop buying mutual funds like this" and "But if I had to start all over again" are mistake-and-regret hooks, consistent with the regret and fear emotions noted in the finfluencer lens.
  • Nothing in the sample covered overlap, capital-gains reporting or switching to direct plans. That could be the sample, or it could mean those topics live more on YouTube, X and forums than on Instagram. I can't tell which.

9. X spot check (small sample, 16 Nov 2026)

Thin evidence. Read-only, in a signed-in browser, using two searches I could read: "mutual fund overlap india" and "regular to direct mutual fund tax switch". A third ("too many funds") loaded but my extraction returned nothing, so it is not counted. The overlap search returned only institutional posts (AMFI, ET Money, NDTV Profit, CNBC-TV18), and the switching search returned about five posts, several from 2023 and 2025. X engagement on these topics was low (tens to low thousands of views). Reddit stayed blocked, so there is still no Reddit evidence.

  • Direct-plan default is live on X. A 2 Nov post on the 1 Nov SEBI change, saying the expense-ratio gap is "mostly distributor commission", got about 4,000 views. This independently confirms the topic and the framing the other lenses found (commission, not fund quality). It does not confirm the circular, which I still haven't seen.
  • "Why is there tax on a switch?" is a recurring confusion. An AMFI-registered distributor explained on 9 Nov that a switch is a redemption plus a new purchase. A 2023 post asked for regular-to-direct switches to be tax-free. A 2025 thread on regular vs direct got about 1,600 views. This supports the switch-cost planner in section 2 (item 3), and shows distributors are already writing explainers on it.
  • Institutional data posts get the reach. CNBC-TV18's October AMFI-data post (equity inflow ₹32,994 cr vs ₹38,293 cr, small-cap inflow down to ₹6,408 cr from ₹9,039 cr) got about 25,700 views, far more than the creator and investor posts I saw. Monthly flow data is a cheap, reliable content trigger.
  • Not found on X in this sample: posts about portfolio overlap from individual investors, tracker complaints and XIRR confusion. The searches were few, so absence here proves nothing.
