Trending topics to Unifolio feature candidates

Indian audience only. Research run of 16 to 19 Nov 2026 (two rounds). Research only: nothing built, committed or pushed. Round 1 was four web-research passes; round 2 added Google Trends India, YouTube, X and Instagram through a logged-in browser, forum and app-review mining, creator feeds, primary-source checks of SEBI/AMFI/RBI documents, and a read-only check of what the repo already has.

1. Read this first

What is now measured, not just described: Google Trends India search interest (relative 0 to 100 per chart, so terms in different charts are not comparable), YouTube view counts on India-specific searches and creator channels, X engagement counts, and real user threads on TradingQ&A, ValuePickr, TechEnclave and the Dhan forum. These samples are still small (about 10 App Store reviews per app, threads of 2 to 20 replies).

Items marked verify were not confirmed at a primary document. Items marked primary were read in the official SEBI, AMFI or RBI document.

What changed since the first version of this report:

  • Overlap is partly built, not "planned". Unifolio already has a household-wide stock-level overlap matrix, but only for equity funds, capped at 12 funds, with no "effective number of funds" figure.
  • The "direct plan default from 1 Nov 2026" has no SEBI circular in anything reachable. It was announced by SEBI's chair on 28 Oct. Do not cite a circular number, and do not assume it covers third-party apps or existing SIPs.
  • The brokerage caps are 6 bps (cash) and 2 bps (derivatives) per the regulation text, not the 5 and 1 bps one article quoted.
  • The SIP "stoppage ratio" of about 116% is not an attrition rate. It divides SIPs ended or completed in a month by new SIPs registered in that month.
  • Demand evidence reorders the ranking. XIRR and "how do I stop my SIP" show the strongest search and video demand; "fund manager change" shows about zero search demand.

9. Compliance guardrails

  • Unifolio is not an RIA. Every feature should show facts about the user's own portfolio, never "switch", "sell" or "buy". The scenario simulator's release gates (markets review, SEBI disclaimer) are the model.
  • Ranked "best funds this month" tables are the format SEBI's finfluencer rules restrict. Do not copy that Instagram format.
  • Educational content from unregistered people may not carry buy, sell or hold calls or return claims. A "claim checker" tool needs a compliance read first.
  • Alerts and digests need a consent purpose that does not exist yet, plus lawyer review of consent texts (already pending).
