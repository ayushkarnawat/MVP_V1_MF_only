# Whole-plan adversarial review of the Batch A candidate (20 Nov 2026)

**Reviewer:** an independent read-only reviewer on the strongest model, briefed with the approved decisions and the project rules but not with my conclusions. **Subject:** `m2-candidate` (Batch A merged, plus the D-2 colour fix) at the time of the review. **Result:** no Critical and no High findings; four Medium and four Low or Medium; one tooling note. Every finding was checked against the code before acting; this file records what was done.

| # | Finding | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
