## Not usable as a benchmark (and why)
- **G-Sec indices** (`NIFTY 10 YR BENCHMARK G-SEC`, `NIFTY 8-13 YR G-SEC`, `NIFTY 4-8 YR G-SEC INDEX`, `NIFTY 11-15 YR G-SEC INDEX`, `NIFTY 15 YR AND ABOVE G-SEC INDEX`, `NIFTY COMPOSITE G-SEC INDEX`): the endpoint returns a PRICE series only, no total-return series. A price series leaves out the interest, so a debt fund compared with it would look better than it is. Stay unregistered until a total-return source is found.
- **Debt, money-market, liquid, short-duration and hybrid composite indices** printed in debt factsheets (CRISIL composites, Nifty Liquid, Low Duration, etc.): not on this endpoint (zero rows). CRISIL's index pages answer (HTTP 200) but offer no download API that was found; a source is still open. Funds in these categories keep "As printed in the fund house's factsheet" until then.
- SDL indices: not in NSE's list.
