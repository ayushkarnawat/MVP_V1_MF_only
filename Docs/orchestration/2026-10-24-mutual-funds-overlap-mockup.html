TriggerBehaviourTiming
Click a Common Holdings rowRow expands in place to show per-fund weight for that stock — same mechanic as the Holdings page's stock table--fast
Click a heatmap cellCell takes a persistent dark border (survives mouse-leave); a detail panel beneath the grid opens/updates showing that pair's shared stocks, titled with both fund names--fast
