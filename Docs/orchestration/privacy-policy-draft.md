## The short version

We know most people won't read every word of a privacy policy, so here is what matters most. The full details follow below.

- **We only collect what we need to show you your mutual fund portfolio.** That means your phone number, your email (if you use it to sign in), your PAN, and the mutual fund statements (CAS) you choose to upload.
- **We can see your investments. We can never touch them.** Unifolio can't buy, sell, redeem or move any investment, and we never ask for your bank details.
- **We never sell your data.** We don't show ads, and we don't use third-party advertising or tracking tools.
- **We'll send you occasional updates and offers.** You can unsubscribe at any time with one tap, and you'll still get important account messages.
- **We store your PAN, encrypted, and never show it in full.** We use it for one thing only: making sure each statement goes to the right person in your family. We never use it for credit checks or KYC, and we never share it.
- **Your statement password is never saved.** We use it once to open the file, then forget it.
- **We keep your insights, not your files.** Uploaded statement files are kept encrypted for 30 days so we can help if something looks wrong, then they're deleted. Your portfolio data stays.
- **Your data is stored in India.**
- **You're in control.** You can view, correct or delete your data at any time, and you can delete your account from inside the app.

Questions? Write to our Grievance Officer at **[privacy@unifolio.in]**.

### 3.2 What's inside a CAS statement

A CAS statement contains the investor's name, PAN, the email and mobile number registered with the fund house, folio numbers, fund names, transaction history, units held, values and the distributor's code (ARN). We use this information only to build and analyse your portfolio. Statements can also list nominees. We do not use nominee details.

### 3.3 Your statement password

CAS statements are password-protected, and the password is usually your PAN. When you enter it, we use it once to open the file. It is **never saved** to our database or our files. If you enter the wrong password, we keep the locked file in temporary memory for up to 15 minutes so you can try again without uploading it again. After that, it is cleared.

### 3.4 Your PAN: what we store and why

Your PAN is the most sensitive thing we keep, so here is exactly how we handle it.

- **What we store.** We store the PAN of each person in your family whose statement you upload. We read it from the statement, or you type it when you complete a family member's details.
- **Why.** Names on statements vary: initials, spellings, nicknames. A PAN lets us reliably put each statement under the right person in your family. It also makes sure the same person's portfolio isn't tracked in two different Unifolio accounts (see section 5).
- **How it's protected.** Each PAN is stored with its own strong encryption, on top of the encryption that protects our whole database. To check for a match, we compare a one-way fingerprint of the PAN that can't be turned back into the PAN itself, so the real PAN is unlocked only when it's genuinely needed.
- **How it's shown.** We never show a PAN in full. In the app you'll only see a masked version, such as **AB\*\*\*\*\*\*4F**.
- **When we check it.** When you upload a statement, we check its PAN straight away so we can tell you then and there whether it belongs to someone already in your family. If you cancel the upload or don't confirm it, any PAN we noted for that upload is released.
- **What we never do with it.** We never use your PAN for credit checks, KYC or any kind of identity verification with outside parties. We never share it with anyone except where the law requires (section 6), and we never use it for marketing.
- **When it's deleted.** A PAN is permanently deleted when you remove that family member, or when your account is deleted. Your own PAN stays for as long as your account exists, because your own portfolio depends on it.

Your uploaded statement file contains your PAN too. That file is deleted after 30 days (section 9).

### 3.5 Information collected automatically

When you sign in, we record basic technical details: your IP address, device type, operating system, browser, and a random device identifier stored by our app on your device. We use these to keep your account secure, spot unusual sign-in activity, fix problems, and understand which devices our users use so we can support them well. We do **not** track your location, and we do not track what you do on other websites or apps.

### 3.6 Public market data

To value your portfolio and calculate analytics, we use publicly available market data such as fund NAVs, expense ratios and index levels from sources including AMFI and the NSE. This is not personal data.

### 3.7 What we never ask for

We will never ask for your bank account details, card details, UPI PIN, Aadhaar number, broker or fund-house login passwords, or access to your contacts, photos or location. **If anyone claiming to be from Unifolio asks for these, it isn't us.**

You can withdraw your consent at any time (see section 10). Withdrawing is as easy as giving consent.

## 5. Data about your family members

Unifolio lets you track your family's investments together. If you upload a statement or add details belonging to someone else, such as a spouse, parent, sibling or child, you confirm that:

- you have their permission, or you are their parent or lawful guardian, to share their information with us for this purpose; and
- you have told them that their portfolio will be visible in your Unifolio account.

For a child under 18, you may share their information only as their parent or lawful guardian. We use a child's data only to show their portfolio within your family view. We never use it for tracking, profiling or advertising.

If you believe your information was added to someone else's account without your permission, contact our Grievance Officer and we will help.

**One person, one account.** For accuracy and privacy, each person's PAN can be linked to only one Unifolio account. If you upload a statement for someone who is already tracked in another Unifolio account, their funds from that statement can count in your family total, but we won't open their individual portfolio to you. We will also never tell you which account they're in.

## 8. How we protect your data

- All data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).
- Our databases and stored statement files are encrypted at rest, with encryption keys managed through AWS's key management service.
- Your PAN is stored with extra, separate encryption. Where we only need to check for a match, we compare a protected one-way fingerprint of it rather than the PAN itself.
- Sign-in codes and session tokens are stored only in scrambled (hashed) form, so even we can't read them.
- Sign-in codes expire after 5 minutes and lock after 5 wrong attempts.
- Access to production systems is limited to authorised team members who need it for their work.

No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach ever happens, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board of India as the DPDP Act requires, and we will tell you what we're doing about it.

## 10. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act, you have the right to:

1. **Access.** Ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it, and a list of who we have shared it with.
2. **Correct and update.** Fix or complete inaccurate data. You can edit most of it directly in the app.
3. **Erase.** Delete your data. You can delete a family member, an import or your whole account from inside the app, or ask us to do it.
4. **Withdraw consent.** Withdraw consent at any time. This doesn't affect anything we did lawfully before you withdrew. Because we need your data to run your portfolio, withdrawing consent will mean closing your account.
5. **Grievance redressal.** Raise a complaint with our Grievance Officer and get a response.
6. **Nominate.** Name someone who can exercise these rights for you if you die or become unable to act yourself.
7. **Complain to the regulator.** If you're not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the **Data Protection Board of India**.

To use any of these rights, write to **[privacy@unifolio.in]** from the email or phone number linked to your account. We may need to verify your identity first. We will respond within [30] days, and in any case within the time the law allows.

- your **display preference** (light or dark mode).

If you clear your browser storage, you'll simply be signed out.

You can stop these messages at any time. Use the "unsubscribe" link in any email, reply STOP to any SMS or WhatsApp message, change your preferences in the app, or write to us. Opting out won't affect your account. You'll still receive the messages you need, such as sign-in codes, security alerts and important notices about your account or these policies.
