Member Profile Completion Map

Detected family members are no longer locked. Their name and PAN are saved with the import, the same as yours. Their dashboards open straight away, and a progress nudge next to their name asks you to fill in the rest.

The change

Today

  • Confirm imports saves a detected member’s PAN in a separate “detected” slot. It only becomes their real PAN when you unlock them.
  • Picking a detected member opens the “Add Ramesh’s details” unlock popup. Their dashboard and analytics stay closed (403) until you choose a relationship.
  • Phone and email can only be added later, through Edit.
  • Name is read-only and always comes from the CAS.

Proposed

  • Confirm imports saves a detected member’s name and real PAN at the same step where your own PAN becomes permanent.
  • Picking any member opens their dashboard and analytics. No lock, no unlock popup.
  • A ring and a “40% complete · Finish profile” chip sit next to their name until the profile reaches 100%.
  • One popup: name (editable), PAN (greyed), relationship, phone and email, all optional. Exit asks before closing.
  • Each Save writes only what you entered. At 100% you see a short success state, then the dashboard.
  • The same data shows in Account → Family members, for you and every member.

Migration 0023Why
Drop details_completed_at, the relationship-required-when-unlocked CHECK, and the trg_member_never_relock triggerNothing is locked any more, and relationship is optional
Add the user_edited value to name_sourceMarks a popup edit, so a later CAS applies the normal name rules instead of the silent replace used for onboarding names, which stay user_entered.
Rename lock_reason → pan_conflict; drop the details_needed valueThe only value left is the other-account case, which drives the banner
Backfill: move each existing detected PAN into pan_encrypted / pan_lookup_hash, unless another account holds itStaging already has locked members

Why there’s no profile_completion column: the % is a count of five fields on the same row, and a stored copy could fall out of step with it.

Decided (1 Oct review)

Name and PAN timingSaved at Confirm imports, alongside Self’s permanent PAN. Relationship, phone and email are saved only on popup Save.
A · No PAN reservation at upload for detected membersTheir PAN is saved only at Confirm imports. Upload still checks each detected PAN against other accounts. If another account takes the PAN during the review, Confirm treats it as the other-account case.
RelationshipOptional. Nothing in the popup is required, except PAN for members whose CAS had none.
Q1 · Editable nameAllowed. On a later CAS, an edited name goes through the same checks as today: a completely different name shows the name-mismatch prompt, a longer variant updates automatically, and a shorter variant is kept. This replaces the 1 Oct “names can’t be edited” rule.
Q2 · Members whose CAS had no PANPAN is editable, required, and counts toward the %.
Q3 · PAN on another accountThe dashboard opens, with a clearly visible red banner. A Save shows a second warning.
Q4 · SelfSelf has a % too. In your own popup, phone and email are read-only, with a “Change in Account Info” link.
Q5 · Weighting and Exit20% per field. Exit asks “Skip completing Ramesh’s profile?” first.
