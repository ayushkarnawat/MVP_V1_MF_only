# Stakeholder message for M3 and M4 (one message; final short version, 24 Nov 2026)
**Send only after the M4 staging deploy has passed its checks (step 9 of the guide and the owner's real export).** If the export or the checklist fails there, delete that item before sending. Same format as the M1 and M2 messages. The "what to check" list for stakeholders, M1 to M4, is at the end.

Unifolio – update

Hi all, the next two sets of improvements (M3 and M4) are now live on our link: https://ls-redesign.unifolio.in

*M3: search and navigation*

* One search box across the Mutual Funds pages, Home, History, Portfolio and Planning (Ctrl+S or Cmd+S opens it): find a fund, fund house, manager, member, SIP, transaction or page and land exactly where it lives. Press Enter to filter the list you are on.
* A slim "On this page" panel on each Mutual Funds tab to jump between sections.
* Dates now read the same everywhere ("Prices as on 12 Nov 2026", monthly data as a month), with a date strip on Allocation and Other Analytics, and one shared "i" pop-up.

*M4: PDF report and checklist*

* A new Export PDF button in the top bar of the Mutual Funds pages. Choose the family or one member, and Full report, This tab or Custom. You get a cover, a contents page with page numbers, your sections laid out for paper, and a notes page. The file is kept for at most 10 minutes and the report never prints a PAN.
* The CAMS import now opens a small floating checklist beside the CAMS site, so you can tick off each step as you go.

*Not in this release:* no numbers changed.

Please send any issue with the screen, what you clicked or searched, and what you expected. For the PDF, tell us which sections you chose and attach the file if you can.

## What stakeholders check, M1 to M4 (short checklist)

**M1: sign-up, sign-in and holdings**
- Sign up and sign in: the resend countdown, the clearer limit message and the wrong-person warning.
- Holdings: full folio numbers with the one hide-all eye, and one slice per category on the donut.
- The four Head of Family emails arrive and read correctly.

**M2: numbers and charts**
- Rankings and category averages use only the same plan and the Growth option, and the two agree. Returns (XIRR) show an "as on" date.
- Home graph: pick up to three benchmarks. The same graph is on the Mutual Funds overview.
- "View all by day's change" pop-up on Home, and the "Watch how it works" video on the import screens.

**M3: search and navigation**
- Search box (Ctrl+S or Cmd+S): try a fund, fund house, member and page, and press Enter to filter each Mutual Funds tab.
- "On this page" side panel jumps to sections.
- Dates and "i" pop-ups: "Prices as on…", monthly data shown as a month, and the date strip on Allocation and Other Analytics.

**M4: PDF report and checklist**
- Export PDF: try Full report, This tab and Custom, for the family and for one member. Check the cover, contents page numbers, header and footer, and notes page, and that no PAN shows.
- Click Export twice quickly: the second should say wait, not error.
- CAMS import: the floating checklist opens beside CAMS and keeps your ticks after a reload. Try Chrome and Edge.

**Always:** check light and dark mode, and send any issue with the screen, what you clicked and what you expected.
