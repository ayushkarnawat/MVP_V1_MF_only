Colour tokens

Source: Unifolio Colour Palette.pdf, adopted for the redesign exactly as Home and the shell do (Home M12 A: scoped, tokens.css untouched). Cards are the palette's card colour (#F5F5F5) on the base (#FCFCFC), with white for popups and modals. Dark values follow the Home workbook.

  • Green is rare (palette): primary button, active tab underline, selected state, focus ring. Decided (M1 B): the fund line is the tier 2 teal, so green stays for UI (primary button, active tab underline, focus ring, logo). Everything else takes colour from tier 2 (teal, indigo, amber) and the semantic set.
  • Semantic colours never fill a card or row: the tint is the background of a small icon disc or chip; full colour only for the icon, the number and a thin border. Small numerals use the AA-safe text shade.
  • Series colours: fund = tier 2 teal #0D9488 (M1 B), benchmark = Chart 03 blue #2563EB, category average = muted grey #9B9B9B. The benchmark colour follows the Home decision.

Chart rules

  • Colours by series, not by rank: fund green, benchmark blue, category grey; legend items are buttons that hide or show a line.
  • Hover shows a crosshair and a tooltip with all series on that date; the legend figures stay the period totals.
  • Lines are rebased to 0% at the period start. Area fill only under the fund line, at low opacity. Axes: 4 ticks, percent, no decimals under 10 steps.
  • Period Returns bars: three per bucket (fund, benchmark, category); bucket width follows the chip (L19). Scatter: grey dots for peers, a larger teal dot with a ring for the fund.
  • Donut colours by rank, largest first, from the palette's chart set; the centre shows the holding's current value.
  • All charts are built from the same series the API returns; no chart invents a point (a gap is a gap).

Click affordances (L14)

Everything with a destination should invite a click without saying so. These are the signals used in every mockup below; hover over anything to see them. Question M7 asks you to approve the set.

Element kindSignal
Cards and tilespointer cursor; lift 1 px with a soft shadow and a darker border on hover; a small arrow appears at the right edge; the card is a button for the keyboard with the green 2 px focus ring
Table and list rowsrow background changes on hover; a chevron appears at the row end
Figures in running text or detailsdotted underline on hover
Chips, legend items, tabscolour change on hover; pressed state is the dark fill (chips) or the green underline (tabs)
Chart elementscrosshair and tooltip on hover; a bar, slice or dot is highlighted and the others dim
Greyed targets (D2 B)text and icons in the disabled grey #C4C4C4 (disabled border #DFDFDF), a "not-allowed" cursor and a "Coming soon" tooltip; no hover lift

Reduced-motion users get the colour and shadow changes without the movement.
