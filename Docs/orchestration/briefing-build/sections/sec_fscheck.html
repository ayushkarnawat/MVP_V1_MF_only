Monthly factsheet check: what we collect, what it holds, and how to run it every month

Item 7 (P/E and P/B) and item 11 (benchmarks) both depend on factsheets. This section records what the factsheets we have actually print, for the houses fetched automatically and the ones imported by hand, and sets out a repeatable monthly check. It is built from a scan of 94 distinct factsheet PDFs and the saved text of the rest (October 2026, mostly 31 Aug and 30 Sep data), plus the live scan of the 11 hand-import files run on 31 Oct. Nothing was read from the VM yet; that is Step 0.

Text or image? Where numbers could hide

  • The 11 hand-import PDFs (live scan, 31 Oct): 1,341 pages, no image-only page. The largest image on any page covers under 0.1% of it (a logo or riskometer). Axis, Bandhan, Trust and WhiteOak have no P/E or P/B lines at all; HDFC and ICICI have glossary text only; JM has commentary only.
  • The 83 cached PDFs of the automatic houses (second scan, 31 Oct): 5,822 pages: 5,764 text pages, 50 with only small images, 8 blank or vector, none image-only, and no image larger than a sliver of a page. Every figure that exists is text. Scheme pages have almost no large images. HSBC and Mirae scheme pages were looked at on screen: charts and riskometers, no P/E or P/B.
  • Not proven: no OCR was run; a figure drawn as vector graphics would not show as an image; most houses' pages were not rendered by eye; Nippon exists only as saved text. The scan script flags any page with large images and little text, so the check is repeatable each month.

Benchmarks

Every house that could be read prints a benchmark per scheme, in text. Label variants: "Benchmark", "Benchmark Name", "Benchmark Index", "Tier 1 Benchmark", "First Tier Benchmark", "Scheme Benchmark", and an "Additional Benchmark" at several houses (HDFC prints both, for example Nifty 100 TRI and BSE Sensex TRI). WhiteOak, UTI and the multi-scheme layouts (Groww, Franklin) need positional extraction. Index funds and ETFs print their tracked index in the same line, for example "Benchmark Nifty 50 TRI" (Edelweiss, Mirae, Navi, Axis), "Tier I Benchmark - Nifty 50 TRI" (Quantum) and "NASDAQ-100 TRI" (Motilal Oswal), which settles D24.

Many are blends: ITI and Invesco print Tier I benchmarks such as "50% NIFTY 500 TRI + 30% ...". A blend is a weighted mix of several indices and needs a stated rebalancing rule or must be shown as not available.

The monthly check (a procedure to repeat)

  1. Day of the job (10th, 06:00 IST). Confirm the cron is installed and the disk has room. Nothing else to run.
  2. Same day or next morning: read the result. grep -E "refresh_fund_managers_monthly|FUND_MANAGER_ALERT" the log. Compare houses processed with last month. Any stale_month, fetch_failed, no_factsheet_link or matching_collapsed is a follow-up. (The job exits 0 even when houses fail, so nothing emails; the missed-month alert is still not built, Priority 1.)
  3. Hand-import the 10 houses the same day (the 45-day freshness check refuses old files): Axis, Bandhan, HDFC (two files), ICICI, Invesco, ITI, JM, Kotak, Trust, WhiteOak. The import command reads managers, and will also read P/E, P/B and benchmark once built.
  4. Keep the files (decided 31 Oct). Use the fetch-and-keep wrapper (Step 0, S2) so the PDFs are saved, and keep them for three months. They are public documents, about 400 to 600 MB a month. Without them nothing can be re-checked.
  5. Run the scan on the month's folder (Step 0, S3) and compare with last month's files.csv: for each house, pages with P/E, pages with P/B, pages with a benchmark, and image-only pages.
  6. Parser-health alerts (to be built with item 7): a house that printed P/E last month and printed none this month; a benchmark count that drops by a fifth; any house with image-only pages (needs OCR); a P/E or P/B outside the sanity range (P/E 3 to 200, P/B 0.2 to 40); a manager or benchmark parse with no match.
  7. Record the month's counts in one small log (houses fetched, houses by hand, funds with a factsheet P/E, funds with a declared benchmark, funds on the fallback). This is the number to watch.

Who: the person who runs the monthly hand import, which is already manual. About an hour a month once the scripts are in the repo. The scan, the keep-files wrapper and the health alerts are written as part of Run 2 and Run 3; until then the scripts in the Step 0 section do the job by hand.
