The 18 funds the category list cannot decide

Four Multi Asset, two Balanced Advantage, one Equity Savings, one Children's fund and ten index funds are decided by one rule: at least 65% of the fund is in Indian shares after hedges. A fund with no holdings data is left out with a plain sentence ("We don't have this fund's holdings yet, so it isn't counted here.").

P/E and P/B, and NSE filings

A wider rescan of the 17 files found no new house with per-fund P/E and P/B; the figure computed from holdings stays the default for every house, with printed values as a cross-check.
