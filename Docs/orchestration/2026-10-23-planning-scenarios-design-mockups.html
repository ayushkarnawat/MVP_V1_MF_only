Planning Design Mockups

Design system, utilities and clickable mockups for the Planning page, with the open questions that came out of writing the documents. Prepared 23 Oct 2026. Nothing is built. Answers save automatically in this page's database; the box at the bottom collects them to paste into chat.

Example numbers. Every figure in the mockups is made up to show the layout. The engine's real numbers will differ, and every standard scenario here shows the same sample figures.
Where we stand (updated 23 Oct): all nine questions are answered and recorded as decisions L36 to L44 in the planning workbook. G1 is C: ScenariosScreen is edited in place with a desktop variant switch (default classic, so mobile is unchanged); the documents are updated to match. The implementation plans wait for your go.
