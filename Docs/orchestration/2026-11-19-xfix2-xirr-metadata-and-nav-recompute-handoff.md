**The change.**
1. `HeldNavResult` gains an additive `filled_scheme_ids: list[uuid.UUID]` (the schemes it wrote a new price for in this run).
2. A small shared helper in `analytics/recompute.py`, for example `async def recompute_households_holding(db, scheme_ids) -> list[uuid.UUID]`: finds the users whose households hold any of those schemes (`Folio.scheme_id` to `HouseholdMember` to user), and for each user does what `scripts/run_analytics_recompute.py` `_run_all` does per user: `try_claim_recompute`, then `recompute_household_analytics`, logging and continuing on failure. **Do not edit `scripts/run_analytics_recompute.py`.**

The 06:00 full run does not (the 06:30 recompute follows it). A recompute failure is logged and **never changes the job's exit code or its NAV summary line** (`NAV_SUMMARY` stays byte-identical in shape; the daily digest parses it). ## Part 2: FX-8e, a holding under a year and flows that net to zero
**Scope (narrowed on purpose, the owner's rule: small radius).** Applies to **the two portfolio tile figures and the per-fund column only**. The fund page, benchmark and window figures are **not changed** (the workbook says "all figures"; that is a wider change, listed for the owner as a follow-up). The existing XIRR values stay as they are; the new fields say how to show them.
**Rule.** The span is the days from a figure's first cash flow to its final flow date (the price date). **Under 365 days = short.**
**The change (additive fields).**
- Tile (`DashboardXirrSummary` and the same response models as Part 1): `lifetime_xirr_basis` and `current_holdings_xirr_basis`, each `"annualised"` or `"since_purchase"`, and `lifetime_since_purchase` and `current_holdings_since_purchase` (strings, the absolute return).
- Per-fund (`FundBenchmarkRow`, the consolidated holding row): `xirr_basis` and `since_purchase_return`.
- **Since-purchase return** = (final value + all money received back) / (all money put in) - 1, from the same flows the XIRR used (same switch and opening-balance handling as the rate: reuse the existing flow builder, do not write a second one). Example: 1,000 in, valued at 1,200 three months later: since-purchase 20% (the annualised figure is about 109%).
- **Flows that net to zero on one day:** when every flow is on one date and the signed flows sum to zero, the rate is `None` (a dash), not `0`. Do this in the callers' wrapper (`portfolio_xirr` / `_portfolio_xirr`), **not in the solver**. Anything else (including a fund bought and valued on the same day with a non-zero sum) is unchanged.
**Tests first:** `test_xirr_short_period.py` (new, same folder): the T8-9 fixtures from `xirr_reference/fixtures.py` for under a year (`since_purchase` basis with the hand-worked value, `annualised` otherwise), `test_three_month_gain_reads_20_percent_since_purchase`, `test_exactly_365_days_is_annualised`, `test_net_zero_on_one_day_gives_no_rate`, and the existing same-day net-zero fixture, which is a strict expected failure from XR-2 (`NET_ZERO_FINDING` in `test_xirr_reference.py`): after Part 2 it should **XPASS(strict)**; report it (the orchestrator removes the marker). Also `test_every_fund_over_a_year_is_unchanged` (a household whose figures are all over 365 days: every number and format equals today's).
