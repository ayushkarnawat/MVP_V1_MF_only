LOCKEDMutual funds only for v1. Confirmed against the backend (asset_class_bucket() only returns Equity/Debt/Hybrid/Other — SEBI MF categories, not real asset classes) and consistent with the parallel Home-screen effort's own MF-only scoping. Direct Stocks, US Stocks, FDs & Bonds, Debt, Gold, and Real Estate appear in the UI because the nav-shell contract already reserves those routes, but render as “Coming soon” — visible, disabled, no data.

2. Mockup round decisions (v1→v8)

Equity: inline expandable group, not a popup

LOCKEDPer nav-shell contract v1.2: Equity is an accordion inline in the sidebar with 4 real child routes (Direct Stocks, Mutual Fund, US Stock, FDs & Bonds). Corrects an earlier mistaken “popup” description.

Total Portfolio Value delta basis

LOCKEDThe number shown is HoldingRow.today_gain, summed across holdings — “Today’s Gain,” not a comparison to any fixed date.

Stocks pie slice split

LOCKEDSplit into Direct Stocks and US Stocks as separate slices, matching the router’s own taxonomy exactly rather than one combined “Stocks” slice.

Member row/segment click

LOCKEDSwitches the page’s own useScope() to that member — same page, same URL, reusing the existing aggregate/member mechanism. No new routing.

Sparkline position

LOCKEDSits beside the Total Portfolio Value number itself, in its own .value-row; “Today’s Gain” moved to its own line below. (v7 had it beside the gain line — corrected in v8.)

Clickability intensity

LOCKEDEvery clickable row/slice/bar gets: resting-state chevron at low opacity, hover background tint + left accent bar + slide, and (pie/bar specifically) a lift + glow. Applied uniformly via a shared .clickable CSS pattern, not per-element one-offs.

Asset Allocation: Filter + Sort, no Search

LOCKEDSort defaults to ascending by allocation %. Filter and Sort buttons added to the card header; Search explicitly excluded from this card.

3. Builder-check findings & resolutions (v9)

A fresh subagent reviewed the written design doc + v8 mockup as a builder with no conversation context, and flagged three places a wrong guess would matter. All three are resolved below.

Finding 1 — pie taxonomy didn’t match the nav-shell contract

FOUNDThe committed contract defines 8 sidebar rows (Mutual Fund + Direct Stocks/US Stocks/FDs & Bonds under Equity + top-level Debt/Gold/Real Estate/Others). v8’s pie had 6 slices, missing Debt and Real Estate.
RESOLVEDMatch the contract exactly. v9’s pie has 8 slices: Mutual Funds, Direct Stocks, US Stocks, FDs & Bonds, Debt, Gold, Real Estate, Others. AllocationBucket returns all 8, non-MF ones with status:"coming_soon".

Finding 2 — Summary Strip XIRR had no disclaimer treatment

FOUNDThe Dashboard’s Lifetime/Current XIRR toggle and opening-cost disclaimer exist specifically because a large opening-balance cost share can badly distort the number (the PR #8 Chandra Tripathi fix). The design doc put a single undisclaimed XIRR figure on this page’s most prominent new surface.
RESOLVEDThe Summary Strip’s XIRR reuses the Dashboard’s full Lifetime/Current toggle + XirrDisclaimerModal system as-is — same component in both places, not a stripped-down number and not a fork. Applies here, on /portfolio/mutual-funds, and anywhere else XIRR is shown.

Finding 3 — member-scope switch can be silently reset

FOUNDPer the contract, scope resets to Family Combined on every page change except between tabs of the same sidebar row. Investments and Mutual Fund are different rows, so picking a member in Section 4 and then clicking into the Mutual Funds slice drops back to Family Combined.
RESOLVEDConfirmed intended — this page follows the same cross-page scope-reset rule as the rest of the shell. No exception built.
