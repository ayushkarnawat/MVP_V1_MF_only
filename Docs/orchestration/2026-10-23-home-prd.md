# PRD: Home (Portfolio Snapshot), MF-only, desktop (draft v1)

**Status:** draft, 2026-10-23. Doc 7. New document; existing PRDs (PRD-01..04) are **not edited**. Where this PRD changes an earlier statement, the conflict is listed in section 9 for the owner. Source of truth: `Docs/Web app UI mockups MFs.pdf` pp. 4-7; decisions L1-L47 and the owner's card answers.

## 1. Problem and goal

Users open the app to answer "how am I doing, what changed, what needs attention" in seconds. The current first screen is the holdings Dashboard. Home gives a family-level snapshot first and sends users deeper through the new navigation.

## 2. Users

Households with one or several members who have imported CAS statements. First-time users reach Home after sign-up, onboarding and CAS upload; returning users after login (L35).

## 3. Scope

**In:** eight-section Home for mutual funds on desktop (greeting, wealth graph with benchmark, rule-based summary, allocation + family bar, what moved today, Worth a look, news; section 6 removed). **Out:** mobile; stocks/ETFs/FDs/gold/real estate data (shown as coming soon); Mira AI; search bar (on hold, L46); market context and cash health (L34); fund-level event cards (L36); changes to existing screens.

## 5. Non-functional

First load about 1.5 s (news loads separately); no new Analytics recompute triggered by Home; no live network in tests; all user-facing strings listed for review before production (F5).

## 6. Success measures and tracking (tracking deferred 2026-10-23)

Share of sessions landing on Home that click into a Portfolio page; Mira popup opens; news click-through. The app has no event tracking today, so success cannot be measured at launch; basic tracking is deferred to PRIORITY 2 in `DEFERRED_FEATURES.md` (requirement H15). Events, consent and privacy design: `2026-10-23-home-tracking-ga4-plan.md`; open questions P1 to P8.

## 7. Risks

News licensing and advice wording (counsel review, dark launch); GDELT reliability and VM access (dry run first); sector view is an estimate; holdings and disclosures are lagged (every number carries an "as of"); disclosure retention limits the 3-month concentration comparison.

## 8. Rollout

Shell PR 1 then PR 2, then `feat/home-snapshot` (backend, frontend), news in the same release and live from day one with the disclaimer (owner, H24 and N5), kill switch kept; counsel review was recommended but is not a gate. Staging QA with the synthetic set and spot checks.
