Motion & interaction spec

TriggerBehaviourTiming
Click the Monthly/Yearly pillChart re-renders with the other bucket size; any open period-drill closes (its period no longer exists at the new granularity)--fast
Click a period's barsThat period's column gets a persistent selected state (bars brighten + scale slightly, label bolds in accent — survives mouse-leave per design.md §11.1); the drill table below re-renders for that period and smooth-scrolls into view--fast
Click a ledger rowRow expands in place (chevron rotates) to show NAV, units, and folio number for that transaction — same in-place expand pattern as Holdings' stock table--fast
Click a type-filter pillLedger re-filters in place; any open row stays open if still in the filtered set--fast
Decided against: a line/area chart for cash flow over time — the owner's reference language ("monthly/yearly buckets," "drill-down to dated transactions") describes discrete periods you click into, which bars represent more literally than a continuous line; bars also give each period an obvious, separately-clickable hit target, which a line's hover-only point doesn't.
Status: Transactions built per design.md §7.4 as a genuine visual redesign — same ledger/drill-down/numbers as InvestmentWithdrawalSection + PeriodBreakdownTable, new layout (stat strip, clickable bar chart, redesigned ledger with type-colored icon chips) per the owner's explicit "still pretty shit, needs more than a port" feedback. Every interactive element satisfies design.md §11.1 from the outset: the clicked chart period keeps a persistent selected look tied to the drill table beneath it (same pattern as Allocation's Round 6 fix and Holdings' fund chips), and ledger rows expand in place rather than updating anything elsewhere on the page.
