Risks

  • Big numbers for young portfolios. The same gain annualises to 109% over three months and 219% over one month, which is why FX-8e hid it. You chose XIRR as default knowingly; the caution chip and the Returns twin are the safeguards. The design PDFs reportedly say to hide XIRR under a year, so a stakeholder may ask.
  • Single transaction (Q1): two things I am reading into your answer, for the backend worker to confirm in the handoff: two purchases on the same day are two flows (so "multiple"), and a switch-in counts as a purchase flow. These now only affect the chip.
  • Accepted limitation (Q2): age is the first flow's date, so a SIP that began with one old tiny buy 13 months ago and is otherwise recent shows no chip.
  • Stale stored results show a dash in Returns until the rebuild (decided to run it immediately).
  • M2 stakeholder message told people the opposite; update it before it is sent.
  • Not verified: I have not run the app or the solver on real flows; the numbers in the sketches are invented. I have not confirmed how switches and bonus units appear in the purchase-flow count.
