Design system: colour tokens

Source: Docs/Unifolio Colour Palette.pdf (16 pages). Values below are read live from the current theme. The palette differs from today's tokens.css in several places; see the questions.

Brand green: rare by design

Primary button, active nav indicator, selected state, logo accent. One or two green elements per screen. Everyday variety uses Tier 2 colours (teal, indigo, amber).

Semantic colours

Never fill a whole card or row. Use the tint as the background, the full-strength colour for the icon and thin border, and the AA-safe text shade for small numbers. Large numerals and chart marks use the primary shade.

Charts

Data colours are assigned by rank, largest holding first (Chart 01 to 12). 15 or more items: top 10 to 11 individually, the rest grouped as "Others".

Benchmark line

The palette has no dedicated benchmark colour. The mockups use Chart 03 blue (#2563EB) so the line is blue as the PDF asks. See question M13.

Chart rules

Line/area chart (new)

  • Green area + line = portfolio rupee value, left axis (compact Cr/L).
  • Blue line = benchmark total return %, right axis, dashed gridlines shared.
  • Hover: crosshair + tooltip with date, value, benchmark %.
  • 1D shows two points and the note "1D compares two NAV days".

Donut (reuse)

  • Existing pie chart with centre label.
  • Coming-soon classes are not drawn.

Bars (reuse)

  • Concentration: two bars (earlier, now).
  • Movers: small diverging bars per row.

Utilities (live)

Pure helpers that the build will unit-test. Type to see the real behaviour.

Other utilities

  • periodRange: period to from/to, clamps to the last 10 years.
  • useHomeSection: loading, error, data hook that cancels on scope change.
  • buildGlanceSentence: the rule-built summary, never contradicts the headline.

One to three items, each with an icon, a headline, one line saying where it is from and when, and one line saying how much of your portfolio it touches.
