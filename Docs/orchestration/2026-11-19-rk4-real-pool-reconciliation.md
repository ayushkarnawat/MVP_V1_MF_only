# RK-4: the app's category pools against the independent AMFI recount, on staging (19 Nov 2026)

**Label (the real-data rule):** run 19 Nov 2026 11:56 IST on the staging database. AMFI's newest price date 18 Nov 2026; **46 of 46 held funds had that day's price** (the NAV job's 06:00 run on 17, 18 and 19 Nov each ended with no fund behind; 17 Nov 06:00 was the first clean unattended run). 14,452 AMFI rows parsed. 19 category-and-plan groups are held (invented test households only). Read-only: the app writes public NAV history while it computes a pool, so everything ran inside a transaction that is always rolled back (nothing persisted). The same AMFI text fed the app and the recount, so every difference below is logic, not timing. Script: `backend/scripts/rk4_reconcile_real_pools.py` on `batch/a-numbers` (the recount: `recount_category_pool.py`).

**What was compared:** for each group, the app's peer set (the pool the category average uses) and its 1Y, 3Y and 5Y ranked pools (the pool "rank out of N" uses) with the recount's pool, which applies the 17 Nov decisions (Growth options only, one series per fund, the holding's plan, no index or ETF, live, with enough history).

## Result
- **Ranked pools (57 comparisons = 19 groups x 3 periods): 40 identical, 17 differ.** The differences sit in **6 groups: Corporate Bond (Direct), Liquid (Direct), ELSS (Direct), Large & Mid Cap (Direct), Mid Cap (Direct and Regular)**. The other 13 groups match fund for fund.
- **Peer sets (the average pool): 4 of 19 identical**; the app's set holds 1 to 4 more funds than the recount's in most groups (reasons below). The Index Funds group has 272 funds in the average pool and none in the rank pool (the index rule: by design).
- **First check-in of the 17 Nov decision "no IDCW in any pool" (T10-6): it fails on real data**: three IDCW series are in ranked pools.
- **End dates (T10-4):** no pool mixes end dates more than 4 days apart today (0 flags in all 19 groups).
- **Recount side:** my first run showed 6 more differences that were the recount's own gap: AMFI also writes the income option as "Income Distribution cum capital withdrawal" and once as "IDWC Option", which the recount did not recognise (Groww ELSS and Multicap, Kotak Infrastructure, TRUSTMF Liquid, Canara Robeco Manufacturing). Fixed test first (`test_spelled_out_and_misspelled_income_options_are_set_aside_as_idcw`); after it those differences closed, so **every difference left is the app's**.

## The four causes (every remaining difference belongs to one)
| # | What happens | Where in the code | Real cases | Effect on a number |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **C1** | A fund whose lowest-coded Growth series has an **unknown plan** is represented by that series. The ranked pool then drops it (it needs the exact plan), so **a real Direct fund is missing from the ranking**. | `scheme_universe.py` `get_category_peers`: accepts a series whose plan is unknown, chooses the Growth series with the lowest code; `performance_ranking.py` `load_ranking_pools` keeps only series of the exact plan | Bank of India Large & Mid Cap (110599 chosen, Direct 119350 lost), Bank of India ELSS (111709 / 119351), Baroda BNP Paribas Corporate Bond (150229 / 150237), Baroda BNP Paribas Liquid (101408 / 119415), Axis Liquid (112713 / 120389) | "Out of N" is one too small in Large & Mid Cap (32 against 33) and Corporate Bond (20 against 21 over 1Y and 3Y); pool members differ in Liquid and ELSS |
| **C2** | **"Growth" is found by looking for the word anywhere in the fund name and option text**, so a fund with "Growth" in its name makes every one of its options look like Growth; the lowest code then wins. | `scheme_universe.py` `rank_key`: `"growth" in text and "idcw" not in text and "dividend" not in text` | Nippon India **Growth** Mid Cap Fund: the **Bonus Option** (118665 Direct, 100376 Regular) is ranked in place of the Growth Option (118668, 100377), in both plans | One fund's return is the bonus option's, not the Growth option's (the two NAVs differ); N unchanged |
| **C3** | A fund with **no Growth series** is represented by its IDCW series, because nothing removes a non-Growth representative. | `get_category_peers` (the representative is whichever series ranks first, even if it is not Growth) | Navi Liquid Fund Direct (Monthly IDCW - Payout, 119161), HSBC Liquid Fund Direct (Daily IDCW, 120037), LIC ULIS (IDCW Reinvestment, 120259, in ELSS) | An IDCW NAV falls at each payout, so its return understates the fund (the code's own comment says so); a daily-IDCW liquid fund sits near zero among its peers. N unchanged, ranks shift by up to the number of such funds |
| **C4** | The **average pool is the peer set**, which still holds the unknown-plan and index series that the ranked pool drops. | `category_ranking.py` `load_category_pool` (peers) against `performance_ranking.py` (the stricter rank pool) | 1 to 4 extra funds per group: Motilal Oswal (several), HSBC Small Cap, PGIM Arbitrage, Navi ELSS Nifty 50 Index and others | The category average includes funds the rank denominator does not; the two figures on one screen describe different sets |
