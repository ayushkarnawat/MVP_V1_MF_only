Left Panel Plan

Planning workbook for the left sidebar of the new Unifolio web app, prepared 22 Oct 2026. Nothing is built, installed or committed. Every claim is marked verified, estimate or unverified. Pick an option on each open card or write your own, then copy your answers at the bottom.

Source of truth: Web app UI mockups MFs.pdf and your decisions in chat. Where an older document disagrees I say so and do not edit it without your yes (card A4).
Scope: the sidebar and what it needs from the shell (router, route files, scope rule, Back). Desktop only. Existing screens, mobile and the backend are not touched. The pages and the top bar's contents are planned separately.

One-minute summary

  • What we are building: a left sidebar with two modes. Main mode has Home, Portfolio, Liabilities and Planning, with Profile and Help & Support at the bottom. Portfolio mode adds Back and Investments, then an Equity group that expands to Direct Stocks, Mutual Funds, US Stocks and FDs & Bonds, then plain Debt, Gold, Real Estate and Others.
  • One new rule from you: opening any Portfolio page always shows Family Combined. Four small questions (B1 to B4) finish it.
  • What changed against the written contract: Equity used to be a page. It is now a group with three new child routes. Cards A2 to A4 handle that.
  • One surprise: react-router has a newer version, 8.5, that needs a newer Node than our CI (card A1). v7 still works.
  • Effort: about 8 developer-days for the sidebar part (estimate), before the second PR that switches the app over.
  • Open: 18 cards. Four of them (A4, D1, D2, D4) are about documents and delivery, two of those you parked.

Risks to keep in view

  • The manager plans against an older contract. Nothing is pushed yet, so fixing it now is cheap. After they build against v1.1 it costs a version bump and a rebase (A4, D4).
  • FDs & Bonds under Equity. This is your call and I follow it. A user may look for FDs under Debt. The route id carries the name, so moving it later changes the URL.
  • The scope rule surprises users. Leaving a member view for Family Combined without notice can confuse someone mid-task. B1 and B2 are there to keep it predictable.
  • Router version. v8 needs Node 22.22; CI and the deploy script use Node 20. Using v7 postpones, not removes, an upgrade (A1).
  • Placeholders reachable by URL. Disabled links hide them in the sidebar only. The shared Coming soon page covers the rest (C4).
  • Duplicate navigation. Equity's children overlap the Investments page's tabs. A page-planning decision, not solved here.
  • Moving state out of MainDashboardFlow (later steps) can change member defaults, banners and the import notice. Port the existing tests first.

To verify before building

  • Where AWS staging builds the frontend and with which Node (A1).
  • Whether <Route lazy> works in declarative mode, in case it is simpler than React.lazy.
  • That a MemoryRouter test with a React.lazy page settles cleanly in Vitest (use findBy queries).
  • Whether a sidebar tint token exists in tokens.css, and its dark value; otherwise one is added.
  • The unreadable item under Mutual Fund in Siddharth's sketch.
  • That the member list still revalidates a session-stored scope after a merge or delete (B4).
  • Whether the Investments tabs and the Equity children should share one source of truth (page planning).

Glossary

TermMeaning here
ShellThe frame around every page: sidebar, top bar, routing and the family-or-member scope.
Route fileOne small file per page that tells the shell its path, label, icon, position and page.
Group rowA sidebar row that opens and closes children and has no page.
ScopeFamily Combined or one member. Every page re-scopes to it.
PlaceholderA route whose page is not built. Links to it are greyed out.
MemoryRouterA router that keeps its location in memory, used to test routed screens without a browser.
Declarative modeThe simplest react-router mode: routes written as components.
