What “no migration” means here

Read first

The analytics sections are precomputed and stored as JSON, one row per user, scope and section. Changing what a section shows means changing that JSON and recomputing it.

In the diagrams: NEW a column or table this change adds · READ an existing column the change reads · PK/FK keys. Unlisted columns exist but are not touched.

Rollout consequence: ship the backend payload change first, let a recompute run, then ship the frontend that reads the new fields. The frontend should fall back to the old layout when a new field is missing.

Open questions

Need your answer before building

Each one changes a mockup above.

Q1 · Item 1

Should switch-outs count as withdrawals? Today they are treated as internal movements.

Q2 · Item 3

A fund with two managers: (a) overlapping percentages with a note, or (b) split equally so they sum to 100%? The mockup assumes (b).

Q3 · Item 4

Per-period columns (recommended) or only a label on today’s numbers?

Q4 · Items 4 and 8

Is “year wise” trailing 1Y/3Y/5Y, or calendar years (how many back)? Should the category average stay AUM-weighted?

Q5 · Items 5 and 8

A real rank per horizon (adds columns), or returns per horizon beside one labelled composite rank?

Q6 · Item 9

1Y/3Y/5Y cost: (a) an estimate at today’s value and TER, or (b) actual past cost from TER history?

Q7 · Item 9

Should the totals row show total yearly cost, equal to the sum of the funds?

Q8 · Item 10

Keep the picker’s date format, or change both screens?
